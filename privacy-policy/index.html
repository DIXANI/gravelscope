Privacy Policy

This policy explains what GravelScope processes when you use the website and how that may change if analytics or advertising are added.

Calculator data

The gravel calculator is designed to run in your browser. The measurements you enter are used to perform the calculation on your device and are not intentionally submitted to GravelScope by the calculator itself.

Server and hosting logs

Like most websites, our hosting and security providers may process basic technical information such as IP address, browser type, request time and pages requested for delivery, security and abuse prevention.

Analytics, cookies and advertising

At initial launch, GravelScope may operate without advertising cookies. If analytics, Google AdSense or another advertising service is added, those services may use cookies or similar technologies as permitted by applicable law. This policy will be updated before or when those services are enabled.

Where legally required, an appropriate consent mechanism will be provided before non-essential cookies are used.

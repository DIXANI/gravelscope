Privacy Policy

This policy explains what GravelScope processes when you use the website, including analytics, cookies and advertising services.

Calculator data

The gravel calculator is designed to run in your browser. The measurements you enter are used to perform the calculation on your device and are not intentionally submitted to GravelScope by the calculator itself.

Server and hosting logs

Like most websites, our hosting and security providers may process basic technical information such as IP address, browser type, request time and pages requested for delivery, security and abuse prevention.

Analytics, cookies and advertising

GravelScope may use analytics services to understand how visitors use the website and Google AdSense to display advertising. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver, measure and, where permitted, personalize ads.

Advertising and analytics providers may receive technical information such as browser details, approximate location derived from an IP address, page activity and advertising identifiers. GravelScope does not receive your Google account password or payment details through these services.

Where required by law, visitors will be offered appropriate consent choices before non-essential cookies or personalized advertising technologies are used. You can also manage Google ad personalization through My Ad Center.

Privacy questions can be sent to hello@gravelscope.website.

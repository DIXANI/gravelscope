Calculator Disclaimer

GravelScope is a planning tool. Read these limitations before using a result to order materials.

Planning estimates only

Results are mathematical estimates based on the dimensions, depth, density and allowance you enter. They do not guarantee the quantity a particular site, supplier or construction specification requires.

Material density varies

Gravel bulk density can vary with rock type, particle size, grading, moisture content, void space and compaction. Preset densities are approximate planning values. Supplier-specific data should take priority.

Site conditions matter

Uneven ground, drainage, settlement, excavation depth, sub-base preparation and compaction can materially change the amount needed. Driveways and structural bases may require professional design or local code compliance.

Cost estimates

Optional cost calculations multiply estimated material weight by the price you enter. They do not automatically include delivery, taxes, minimum order quantities, disposal, equipment, labor or other project charges.

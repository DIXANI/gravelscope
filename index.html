How to Calculate How Much Gravel You Need

Gravel is measured by volume for the project area, but sold by weight, bulk volume or bag. This gravel volume calculator works in three steps.

Measure the project area.Enter the length and width, circle diameter, or an area you already know.
Choose the finished gravel depth.Use the depth your project requires rather than guessing from the total material pile.
Select the material and allowance.Density converts volume into weight. Add an allowance for waste, settlement or compaction when appropriate.
Formula: volume = area × depth. Estimated weight = adjusted volume × bulk density. Gravel density varies with stone size, moisture and grading, so use your supplier's density whenever they provide one.

Already know the square footage? Switch to Known area and type it in. If the area is round, use Circle and enter the diameter instead.

Gravel calculator yards

A cubic yard is 27 cubic feet, so dividing the cubic feet of your layer by 27 gives yards. The calculator does this for US and metric input.

Gravel calculator tons

Tons measure weight, so the figure depends on density: the same volume of river rock weighs more than pea gravel, which is why the tonnage changes when you change the gravel type.

Gravel calculator cubic feet

Cubic feet suit bags and small jobs. A 50 lb bag holds roughly 0.5 ft³, so 36.7 ft³ comes to about 74 bags.

20 kg gravel bag coverage

One 20 kg bag covers roughly 0.25 m² at 50 mm depth — about four bags per square metre. Coverage halves as depth doubles, so 100 mm needs around eight bags per square metre and a 10 m² path at 50 mm takes about 40 bags.

Worked Example

A 20 ft × 10 ft area with a 2 in layer gives a base volume of about 33.3 cubic feet. Adding a 10% allowance gives about 36.7 cubic feet, or about 1.36 cubic yards, and the weight then depends on the chosen bulk density. That is why changing the gravel type can change the tonnage without changing the dimensions.

Gravel Calculator FAQ

Common questions about how the calculator works and how much gravel to order. For materials and grades, see types of gravel and sizes.

What is the GravelScope gravel calculator?

It is a free online gravel calculator. Enter the area and depth of your project and it returns the volume in cubic yards, cubic feet or cubic metres, the estimated weight in tons, tonnes or kilograms, the number of bags, and an optional material cost.

How to calculate gravel?

Work out the volume first with volume = area × depth, then convert to weight with weight = volume × bulk density. For example, 200 sq ft at 2 in deep is about 33.3 cubic feet, which is roughly 1.83 short tons at a density of 100 lb/ft³.

How much gravel do I need?

Measure the length and width (or the square footage) of the area, choose the finished depth, and multiply. Enter those figures in the calculator and it adds your waste allowance and converts the result into tons, yards and bags for the material you select.

Why should I add a waste or compaction allowance?

An allowance covers uneven ground, spreading loss, settlement, compaction and measurement error. It is adjustable because the right percentage depends on the job — around 5–10% for a small flat bed, and more for a deep driveway base.

What is 1 ton of gravel?

A US short ton is 2,000 lb (about 907 kg). Gravel weighs roughly 1.4–1.6 tons per cubic yard, so one short ton is about 0.6–0.75 cubic yards, or roughly 16–20 cubic feet.

How to calculate gravel per ton?

Divide the weight you need by the weight of one cubic yard of the material. If your gravel weighs 1.5 tons per cubic yard, then 6 tons is 6 ÷ 1.5 = 4 cubic yards. Enter a supplier density as a custom value to convert volume and weight accurately.

How much will 10 tons of gravel cover?

Coverage depends on depth. At about 1.5 tons per cubic yard, 10 tons is roughly 6.7 cubic yards (about 180 cubic feet). That covers around 1,080 sq ft at 2 in deep, about 720 sq ft at 3 in, or about 540 sq ft at 4 in.

How much is a m3 of gravel?

A cubic metre is a volume, not a price. It weighs roughly 1.5–1.7 tonnes (about 1,500–1,700 kg) depending on the stone. Cost varies by material and supplier, so enter a price per ton in the calculator to estimate your own material cost.

How do I calculate gravel for a driveway?

Measure the driveway length and width, enter the planned layer depth, then pick the closest material. The correct base and compaction depend on soil, drainage and vehicle load, so follow local contractor or engineering guidance for the layers.

How many bags of gravel do I need?

Enter the bag size your supplier uses. GravelScope divides the estimated weight by the bag weight and rounds up to the next whole bag.

How much does 20 kg of gravel cover?

A 20 kg bag holds about 0.0125 m³, covering roughly 0.25 m² at 50 mm depth — about four bags per square metre. At 100 mm, coverage halves to about eight bags per square metre.

What is the difference between gravel calculator yards and tons?

Cubic yards measure volume and tons measure weight. A cubic yard of gravel typically weighs 1.4–1.8 tons depending on the material, so the same area can need a different tonnage for pea gravel than for crushed stone.

Can I use it as a pea gravel calculator or crushed gravel calculator?

Yes. Choose Pea gravel, Crushed stone or River rock, or enter a custom density from your supplier, and the calculator converts the project volume into weight.

How accurate is this gravel calculator?

The geometry is calculated directly from the dimensions you enter. Weight is an estimate, because bulk density varies with material, supplier, moisture and grading — replace the preset with supplier data when you order.

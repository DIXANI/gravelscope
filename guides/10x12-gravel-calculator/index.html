How to Calculate Gravel for 10x12 Area

Use this formula for any size area:

Formula: Cubic Yards = (Length × Width × Depth in feet) ÷ 27

For 10x12: (10 × 12 × depth in feet) ÷ 27

Step-by-Step 10x12 Gravel Calculation

1
Calculate area. 10 ft × 12 ft = 120 sq ft
2
Convert depth to feet. 2 inches = 0.167 ft, 4 inches = 0.333 ft, 6 inches = 0.5 ft
3
Calculate volume. 120 sq ft × depth in feet = cubic feet
4
Convert to cubic yards. Divide cubic feet by 27 to get cubic yards

Example: 10x12 Area with 4 Inch Depth

  1. Area: 10 × 12 = 120 sq ft
  2. Depth: 4 inches = 0.333 ft
  3. Volume: 120 × 0.333 = 40 cubic feet
  4. Cubic yards: 40 ÷ 27 = 1.48 yd³ (base)
  5. With 10% allowance: 1.48 × 1.1 = 1.63 yd³

For practical ordering, round up to 1.75 cubic yards or 2.5 tons.

10x12 Gravel Calculator Common Questions

How much gravel for a 10x12 area with 2 inches?

You need approximately 0.89 cubic yards, which is about 1.2 tons of gravel. This equals roughly 43 bags of 50lb gravel. Always add 10% extra for waste.

Is 2 inches of gravel enough for 10x12?

2 inches is enough for decorative gravel beds and light foot traffic. For driveways or high-traffic areas, use 4-6 inches minimum.

How much does 10x12 gravel cost?

Cost depends on material type and local prices. Typical costs: $40-60 per ton for material, plus delivery. A 10x12 area with 4 inches depth costs approximately $90-150 for material.

Can I use pea gravel for 10x12 area?

Yes, pea gravel works well for 10x12 decorative areas, paths, and patios. Use 3-4 inches depth for foot traffic, 2 inches for decorative beds.

How many tons per cubic yard for 10x12?

Most gravel weighs 1.5-1.6 tons per cubic yard. For a 10x12 area, 1 cubic yard covers about 13.5 sq ft at 2 inches depth.
